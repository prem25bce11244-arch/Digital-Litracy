## What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cybercrime in which an attacker disguises themselves as a trusted entity — such as a bank, government body, or popular website — to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information like passwords, OTPs, or financial details.

---

## Case Study: The Fake Bank Alert (Realistic Fictional Scenario)

Ravi, a 19-year-old engineering student in Bhopal, received a WhatsApp message claiming to be from his bank. The message stated that his account had been temporarily blocked due to a KYC update failure and asked him to click a link to "re-verify" his details within 24 hours, or face account suspension.

**Step-by-step breakdown of how the attack worked:**

1. **Bait:** Ravi received an urgent-sounding message with a link that looked almost identical to his bank's official website URL (e.g., `sbi-update-kyc.net` instead of `sbi.co.in`).
2. **Hook:** Panicked by the threat of losing access to his account, Ravi clicked the link and was taken to a fake login page.
3. **Data Capture:** He entered his account number, password, and the OTP he received on his phone. All three were instantly captured by the attacker's server.
4. **Execution:** Within minutes, ₹18,500 was transferred out of his account via UPI.
5. **Realisation:** Ravi noticed the transaction alert and contacted his bank, but the money was already gone.

**Who is typically targeted?**
First-time internet users, college students, senior citizens, and anyone unfamiliar with how banking websites and official communications work.

**Consequences:**
Financial loss, emotional distress, compromised personal data, and potential identity theft. Legal recovery of funds in such cases is often difficult and slow.
