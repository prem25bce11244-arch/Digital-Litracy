## 🛡️ General Online Safety

| # | Prevention Tip |
|---|----------------|
| 1 | **Use strong, unique passwords** for every account. A strong password contains at least 8 characters with a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse the same password across platforms. |
| 2 | **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** on all important accounts including email, GitHub, LinkedIn, and banking apps. This adds an extra layer of security beyond your password. |
| 3 | **Never click on suspicious links** received via SMS, WhatsApp, email, or social media — even if they appear to come from a known contact or official organisation. |
| 4 | **Verify the website URL** before entering any login details. Genuine websites use HTTPS and have correct spelling (e.g., `sbi.co.in`, not `sbi-kyc-update.com`). |
| 5 | **Keep your devices and apps updated.** Software updates often include security patches that protect against known vulnerabilities and malware. |
| 6 | **Avoid using public Wi-Fi** for logging into banking apps, email, or any sensitive accounts. If necessary, use a VPN (Virtual Private Network). |

## 💳 Financial & UPI Safety (Mandatory Tips)

| # | UPI / Financial Safety Tip |
|---|---------------------------|
| 7 | **Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank password** with anyone — not even someone claiming to be from your bank, telecom provider, or a government helpline. Legitimate organisations will never ask for these. |
| 8 | **Be alert to "Receive Money" scams on UPI.** Scammers often send a collect/payment request framed as "I'm sending you money — please approve." Approving such requests deducts money from your account, not theirs. Always read UPI prompts carefully before confirming. |

## 📢 How to Report Cybercrime in India

If you or someone you know is a victim of cybercrime:

- 🌐 **Online:** File a complaint at [https://cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in)
- 📞 **Helpline:** Call **1930** (available 24x7 – National Cyber Crime Helpline)
- 🏛️ **Local Police:** Visit your nearest police station and file an FIR under the IT Act, 2000

> **Important:** Report financial fraud immediately. Banks can sometimes freeze or reverse transactions if reported within a few hours.
