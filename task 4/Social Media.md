
### Task 4 – Email Etiquette | CSE0001 Digital Literacy


## ✅ DO's

| # | Do                                                                                 |
|---|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | **Keep your profile professional.** Use a clear, appropriate profile photo and a bio that reflects your academic and career interests. |
| 2 | **Think before you post.** Ask yourself: would you be comfortable if your professor or future employer saw this? |
| 3 | **Use privacy settings.** Regularly review who can see your posts, personal details, and friend/connection lists. |
| 4 | **Credit others' work.** Always give proper credit when you share someone else's content, photos, or ideas. |
| 5 | **Report harmful content.** If you encounter cyberbullying, harassment, or misinformation, use the platform's reporting tools. |
| 6 | **Engage positively.** Use social media to share knowledge, celebrate others' achievements, and build a supportive online community. |
| 7 | **Verify before sharing.** Always check whether a news article, image, or claim is credible before sharing it with others. |

---

## ❌ DON'Ts

| # | Don't                                                                              |
|---|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | **Don't share personal information publicly.** Avoid posting your phone number, home address, college ID, or daily schedule. |
| 2 | **Don't engage in cyberbullying.** Never mock, harass, or make offensive comments about others online — regardless of whether they are strangers or peers. |
| 3 | **Don't post in the heat of the moment.** Avoid venting frustrations about classmates, professors, or institutions online — it can have lasting consequences. |
| 4 | **Don't accept friend/follow requests from strangers.** Unknown accounts may be fake profiles used for phishing, scamming, or data collection. |
| 5 | **Don't share login credentials or OTPs.** No legitimate platform, person, or organisation will ever ask for your password or OTP via social media or chat. |
| 6 | **Don't ignore suspicious links.** If someone sends you an unexpected link, especially from an unfamiliar account, do not click it. |
| 7 | **Don't overshare your location.** Avoid live location sharing or posting real-time updates about where you are. |
